The Three Echelons

The population of Noctura is organized across three distinct metaphysical and operational tiers, defined by lifespan, leverage, and biological vulnerability.

I

Mortals

Divided into two operational camps: the Anchored and the Awake.

The Anchored

Mundane humans who crossed the Drift by accident and stayed, completely unaware of the reality surrounding them. Noctura’s ambient glamour induces a continuous cognitive dissonance; when confronted with impossible phenomena, their minds instinctively rationalize the event (a lycanthrope is "a rabid bear," a vampire’s speed is "a trick of the streetlamp," and an occult ward is "quaint local theater.") They staff town hall, run coffee shops, patch utility lines, stock hardware stores, and maintain the vital illusion of small-town normalcy.

The Awake

Humans who have pierced the glamour and seen behind the curtain. Possessing no innate magic, they survive on pure grit, cold iron, perception, and leverage, exploiting all that human ingenuity and tradecraft have to offer. They act as the indispensable shock absorbers and intermediaries managing the boundary between the aware and unaware.

II

Supernaturals

Witches, bloodline sorcerers, shapeshifters, mediumistic lineages, and entities carrying active metaphysical traits. While formidable, they remain biologically mortal and susceptible to natural aging, iron, silver, ballistic trauma, and physical exhaustion.

III

Immortals

Ancient vampires, unbound fae, and ageless relics. Immune to time and biological decay, they are detached, predatory, and politically lethal.
