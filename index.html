THE FOUNDING OF NOCTURA

Seven strangers, drawn to an isolated stretch of coast.

The Salem witch trials had ended, but the suspicion and violence that fueled them had not. Witches, shapeshifters, vampires, and other beings continued to live under the threat of discovery and annihilation.

The land possessed a strange, persistent influence. Travelers found themselves returning to places they had already passed. Paths seemed to change when no one was watching, and the boundary between the familiar world and something older felt unusually thin.

Together, they began to imagine something none had originally come seeking: a permanent sanctuary where mortals and supernatural beings might live alongside one another without fear of discovery.

THE SEAM

The Drift

The Drift is the invisible, non-Euclidean seam separating Noctura from linear Massachusetts geography. Navigable by intent for those aware of the supernatural, mundane travelers cross it purely by accident, only realizing they have slipped out of reality when the asphalt behind them terminates into brackish marshwater and black cedar tangles. Crossing the threshold immediately shears overhead geometry, dropping commercial GPS into open-ocean dead zones and severing external telecom. While Aware entities traverse the boundary freely, mundane mortals crossing back outward have their memories blunted by the Undertow, dissolving impossible encounters into the vague fog of an ordinary seaside drive.

THE ANCHOR

The Undertow

The Undertow is the sedative, low-frequency psychic pressure radiating from Noctura’s ancient leylines. Operating like an invisible metaphysical anchor, it quietly saps the momentum to leave. For mundane mortals, it induces an unnatural, placid calm that dissolves the urgency of outside jobs, unpaid bills, or abandoned families, while smoothing traumatic horrors into easily dismissed background noise.

For supernaturals, it feels like the abrupt silencing of a lifetime of ambient static: a deadened, peaceful sanctuary where instinct can breathe without paranoia. Where nature can thrive.

Breaking its grip requires deliberate intention, and any mortal who crosses back out carries a lingering mental haze that softens the entire town into the unremarkable, generic blur of an ordinary trip.
