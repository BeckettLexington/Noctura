KINDS OF BEING

Mortals

Not everyone in Noctura is supernatural. Mortals live, work, build families, and exist alongside the supernatural population—whether they understand the world around them or not.

✦Anchored

Ordinary mortals who remain subject to the Undertow and its influence. Their perception and understanding of Noctura's supernatural reality is limited by the forces that keep the hidden world hidden.

✦Awake

Mortals who are aware of Noctura's supernatural reality. They can knowingly interact with supernatural society and understand the truth of the world around them, while remaining entirely human.

Important

Being Awake does not give a mortal supernatural powers or abilities. They are still human. The difference is what they are capable of knowing and perceiving.
