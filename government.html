MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENT

An ordinary town, governed in the ordinary way.

Since the Municipal Charter of 1823, Noctura has been governed by an elected mayor and Town Assembly, with mortal and supernatural residents eligible for municipal office. Nathaniel Whitcomb, an Awake mortal merchant, was its first elected mayor.

Town Hall oversees municipal life: public safety, the town clinic, and the courts that hear ordinary matters of law. Most of the people who staff it are Anchored, and many have no idea what else shares the town with them.
